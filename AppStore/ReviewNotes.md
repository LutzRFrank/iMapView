No account is required to use the app.

Version 1.3.1 adds a map style selector and updates the analog clock. The app supports iPhone, iPhone Duo, and iPad.

To review the map styles and clock, launch the app and dismiss the welcome screen with “Los geht’s.”

1. Tap the map icon next to Help. Switch between Standard, Hybrid, and Satellit (Satellite).
2. Check the translucent clock dial and its red second hand. Use the handle at the top to drag the card.
3. Select a city in the bottom strip to see the analog and digital clocks update to that city’s local time.

To review iPhone Duo support:

1. Launch on iPhone Duo and dismiss the welcome screen with “Los geht’s.”
2. Open the inner display. In wide windows the clock card starts at the leading edge, leaving the crosshair visible.
3. Drag the card using the handle at its top. Fold, unfold, or rotate the device; its position is constrained to the available card area as the window changes size.
4. Select a city using the bottom strip. The map and selected time zone are kept in the same view state during resizing.
5. Open Help using the question-mark button in the system toolbar.

To review the day and night display:

1. Launch the app and dismiss the welcome screen by tapping “Los geht’s.”
2. Zoom out on the world map or use the strip at the bottom to jump to a city currently on the night side.
3. The night side appears dark blue, with a smooth twilight gradient between day and night.

The display is calculated entirely on device from the current date and time and updates once per minute. The app does not request location access. Apple MapKit and Core Location are used for map content and place-name lookup.
