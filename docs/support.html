Find your way around.

Move the map, follow the crosshair, and let iMap View do the time-zone math.

Using the map

Drag or zoom the map to choose a place. The card updates with its local time, place name, time-zone name, UTC offset, and boundary. Use a city shortcut to jump there instantly.

Place or boundary not updating?

Place lookup uses Apple’s reverse-geocoding service and may need an internet connection. Time-zone boundary geometry is bundled with the app and works offline.

Still need help?

Open a public GitHub issue with your device model, iOS version, and the steps that reproduce the problem.
