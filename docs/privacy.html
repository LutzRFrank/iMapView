Privacy · Updated July 21, 2026

Your map stays yours.

No accounts. No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

Data collected by AtlasView

AtlasView does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data. It has no developer-controlled server and contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Map and place lookup

The app uses Apple MapKit and Apple’s reverse-geocoding service to identify the place and time zone beneath the map crosshair. These requests are handled by Apple and may require an internet connection. AtlasView does not request or use your current GPS location.

Data on your device

The current version does not persist map positions, selected locations, or usage history. Bundled time-zone geometry contains no personal information.

Questions

For privacy questions, open an issue in the AtlasView GitHub repository.
